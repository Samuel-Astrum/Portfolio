About Me

Hi! My name is Samuel Miller, and I am a recent Computer Science graduate from the Georgia Institute of Technology with a specialization in Media and People. Throughout my educational career, I looked to learn at the intersection between technical development and human-centered design, creating projects and focusing my intentions on creating ideas that not only solve a problem but are accessible and enjoyable to use. My primary toolset has varied between both front and back-end languages, including Java, Typescript/Javascript, Python, and C/C#. I have experience in both building solo projects as well as larger, team-oriented projects that involve communication, version controlling, teamwork and management. In addition to software development, I have developed the skills for data analysis and data science roles, covering statistics, discrete mathematics, calculus, linear algebra and combinatorics.

In terms of career development, I am looking to work across a broad range of career paths that allow me to utilize my skills. In addition to Software Development/Engineering, I am interested in data science/visualization roles, as well as IT roles that allow me to utilize my people-centered skills. Beyond my career in Computer Science, I maintain an interest in video games, representing Georgia Tech as part of their eSports team. I additionally find interest in tennis, and visual arts, involving painting, drawing, and digital artforms. As I pursue my technological career, my overarching goal is to utilize my creative history and thought process to be a vital perspective for any industry.
